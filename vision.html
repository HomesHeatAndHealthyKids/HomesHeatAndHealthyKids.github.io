Our Vision

A warm and healthy environment for every child.

A child's home environment is one of the key determinants of their life-long health. If their home is under-heated, children are at a higher risker of these health complications, particularly respiratory illness. With a broad range of expertise, from research to lived experience, our team is setting out to answer some important questions that can help to ensure that no child is at risk in their own home.

Our Big Questions

How many respiratory illnesses are due to under-heated homes?
